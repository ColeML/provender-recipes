Baked Mac and Cheese

Serves 12 · 55 min total

Americanvegetariankid-friendlypotluck

Ingredients

  • 16 oz macaroni — uncooked
  • 2 cup chicken broth
  • 2 cup heavy cream
  • 2½ cup cheddar cheese — shredded, divided
  • 2 cup Italian cheese blend — shredded
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup Panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning — divided
  • 1 tsp garlic powder — divided
  • ¾ tsp smoked paprika — divided
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp black pepper — divided
  • 1½ tsp salt — divided

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375F. Add the chicken broth, heavy cream, Italian seasoning, half the garlic powder, half the smoked paprika, cayenne, salt, and pepper to a sauce pot. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, whisking occasionally.
  2. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer 7 minutes, whisking often, to reduce.
  3. Remove sauce from heat. Combine the shredded cheddar and Italian cheese blend in a bowl, reserving about a cup for layering. Add the rest to the sauce a handful at a time, whisking until smooth.
  4. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the macaroni and boil until tender, about 7 minutes; drain.
  5. While the macaroni boils, make the breadcrumbs: melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat, add the Panko, remaining Italian seasoning, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook about 3 minutes until toasted; cool slightly.
  6. Stir the drained macaroni into the cheese sauce. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  7. Layer half the macaroni in the dish, sprinkle the reserved cheese over it, then layer the remaining macaroni on top.
  8. Combine the cooled breadcrumbs with the topping cheddar and sprinkle over the top.
  9. Bake at 375F for about 20-25 minutes until bubbling at the edges and golden on top. Cool 5 minutes before serving.

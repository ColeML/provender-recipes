Banana Pudding

Serves 8 · 260 min total

dessertno-bakemake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 11 oz vanilla wafer cookies — about 1 box
  • 1 box vanilla instant pudding mix — 3.4 oz
  • 2 cup cold milk — for the pudding
  • 4 ea ripe bananas — thinly sliced, plus extra for garnish
  • 8 oz frozen whipped topping — Cool Whip, thawed

Steps

  1. Whisk the pudding mix with the cold milk per the package; refrigerate to set.
  2. Layer half the wafers in an 8-inch square dish, spread half the pudding over them, then a layer of banana slices. Repeat: wafers, pudding, bananas.
  3. Top with the whipped topping. Refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
  4. Garnish with extra wafers and banana slices just before serving.
  5. Make-ahead: best made the night before so the wafers soften into a cake-like texture. No bake.

Beef and Cabbage Stir Fry

Serves 8 · 30 min total

Asianquickone-potkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 head green cabbage — finely shredded
  • 4 ea carrots — peeled and shredded
  • 6 ea green onions — sliced
  • 4 clove garlic — minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger — grated
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds — garnish
  • salt and pepper — to taste

Steps

  1. Make the stir fry sauce: stir together the soy sauce, sesame oil, sriracha, and brown sugar in a small bowl.
  2. Prep the vegetables: finely shred the cabbage, peel and shred the carrots, slice the green onions, mince the garlic, and grate the ginger.
  3. Heat a large skillet or wok over medium heat. Add the cooking oil, ground beef, garlic, ginger, salt, and pepper; cook until the beef is browned, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the cabbage and carrots; stir and cook until slightly wilted, then stir in the sauce and green onions.
  5. Garnish with sesame seeds and a drizzle of sriracha. Serve over rice-cooker jasmine rice.

Blackened Chicken Breast

Serves 8 · 17 min total

cajunquickgluten-free

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breast — boneless, skinless
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp onion powder
  • 2 tbsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp salt — pantry staple
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 ea lemon — cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp parsley — chopped, for garnish

Steps

  1. Adapted from a blackened mahi mahi recipe, swapping in chicken breast to keep this night budget-friendly.
  2. Mix the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, thyme, cayenne, basil, oregano, and salt in a small bowl for the blackened seasoning.
  3. Slice or pound the chicken breasts to an even thickness. Pat dry, then rub the seasoning over both sides.
  4. Melt butter in a cast-iron or other heavy skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. Add the chicken and cook for 5-6 minutes per side, until it reaches 165F internally.
  6. Serve with lemon wedges and a sprinkle of fresh parsley.

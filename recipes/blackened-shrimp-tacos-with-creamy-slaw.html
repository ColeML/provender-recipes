Blackened Shrimp Tacos with Creamy Slaw

Serves 4 · 35 min total

tex-mexseafoodquickno-oven

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb shrimp — peeled and deveined, 31/40 count; dial the cayenne down for the kids
  • 1 bag coleslaw mix — shredded cabbage and carrots
  • ¾ cup mayonnaise — split between slaw dressing and garlic-lime sauce
  • ½ Tbsp honey — for slaw dressing
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard — for slaw dressing
  • 1 tsp red wine vinegar — for slaw dressing
  • 2 whole lime — juice for sauce plus wedges to serve
  • 1 bunch green onions — sliced, for slaw and garnish
  • 1¼ Tbsp smoked paprika — blackening seasoning plus a pinch in the sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper — reduce for kids
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • 2 Tbsp butter — for cooking the shrimp
  • 2 clove garlic — minced
  • 12 whole corn tortillas — small, toasted
  • salt — to taste
  • black pepper — to taste

Steps

  1. Make the slaw: whisk 1/3 cup mayonnaise with the honey, Dijon, red wine vinegar, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Toss with the coleslaw mix and half the green onions. Refrigerate.
  2. Make the sauce: stir the remaining 1/3 cup mayonnaise with the garlic powder, a pinch of smoked paprika, salt, and the juice of half a lime.
  3. Toast the tortillas in a dry skillet until browned at the edges; keep warm under a towel.
  4. Make the blackening seasoning: combine 1 Tbsp smoked paprika, thyme, oregano, cumin, cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Toss with the shrimp until coated.
  5. Melt the butter with the minced garlic in a large skillet over medium heat, about 1 minute. Add the shrimp and cook just until firm and opaque, 3-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
  6. Build each taco: slaw, a few shrimp, a drizzle of garlic-lime sauce, sliced green onion, and a squeeze of lime.

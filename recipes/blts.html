BLTs

Serves 4 · 22 min total

americansandwichquickno-oven

Ingredients

  • 1 lb bacon — cooked crispy on the griddle or in a skillet (no oven this week)
  • ½ cup mayonnaise — for spreading and toasting the bread
  • 8 slice sandwich bread — 2 slices per sandwich
  • 1 head iceberg lettuce — small head; torn into pieces
  • 2 whole slicer tomato — sliced; blot if very juicy
  • salt — to taste
  • black pepper — freshly cracked, to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the bacon on the griddle or in a skillet over medium heat until crispy, about 8-10 minutes, turning once. Drain on paper towels. (No oven this week.)
  2. Lightly spread mayonnaise on both sides of all 8 bread slices, about 1 Tbsp per 2 slices.
  3. Toast the bread in a hot dry skillet or on the griddle until golden and crisp, about 3 minutes per side.
  4. Spread the remaining mayonnaise on one side of each toasted slice.
  5. Build each sandwich: lettuce, then bacon (3-4 slices, crumbled), then tomato with a pinch of salt and pepper. Top with the second slice, mayo side down, and serve.
  6. Best made fresh; not a great next-day leftover.

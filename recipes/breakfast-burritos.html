Breakfast Burritos

Serves 8 · 50 min total

americanbreakfastkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 8 oz cheddar cheese
  • 1 ea onion — yellow
  • 1 ea bell pepper
  • 2 tbsp butter — divided
  • 1 lb ham — cooked, diced
  • 12 large egg
  • 8 ea flour tortillas — burrito-size

Steps

  1. Shred the cheddar if not pre-shredded. Dice the onion and bell pepper.
  2. Saute the onion and bell pepper in butter until soft; season with salt and pepper and set aside.
  3. Dice the ham and saute in butter until browned; set aside.
  4. Whisk the eggs and scramble them in butter until just set.
  5. Fill each tortilla with eggs, peppers and onions, cheese, and ham, then roll up and serve warm.

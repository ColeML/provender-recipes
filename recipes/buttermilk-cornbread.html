Buttermilk Cornbread

Serves 9 · 35 min total

americanvegetarian

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup unsalted butter — melted
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • ⅓ cup light brown sugar
  • 2½ tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 large egg — lightly beaten
  • ⅔ cup buttermilk — from the half-gallon on hand
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup cornmeal
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt — pantry staple

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350F and line an 8x8 pan with parchment or grease and flour.
  2. Combine melted butter and oil in a large bowl and stir well.
  3. Add sugars and stir until combined. Add eggs and buttermilk and stir well.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, cornmeal, baking powder, and salt.
  5. Add dry ingredients to wet and stir until just combined.
  6. Spread into the prepared pan and bake for 25 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.

Buttermilk Fried Chicken Tenders

Serves 8 · 95 min total

americankid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 4 cup buttermilk — for marinade; from the half-gallon on hand
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp salt — pantry staple
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 2½ lb chicken tenderloins
  • 4 cup vegetable oil — for frying
  • 2 cup all-purpose flour — divided
  • 4 cup panko breadcrumbs

Steps

  1. Whisk together buttermilk, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, paprika and pepper in a large bowl. Add the chicken tenders and stir to coat.
  2. Cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 1 hour or up to 6 hours.
  3. Heat 4 inches of vegetable oil in a deep frying pan or Dutch oven to 300F over medium heat.
  4. Remove the chicken from the marinade, reserving some marinade and stirring in half the flour until combined.
  5. Add the remaining flour to a shallow bowl and the panko to a separate shallow bowl.
  6. Dredge each tender in the dry flour, then the buttermilk mixture, then the panko, pressing to coat.
  7. Fry the tenders (3-4 at a time) for 5-6 minutes until golden and 165F internally.
  8. Drain on a cooling rack to keep them crispy.
  9. Serve with dipping sauce.

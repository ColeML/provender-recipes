Buttermilk Pancakes

Serves 8 · 15 min total

americanbreakfast-for-dinnerkid-friendlyquick

Ingredients

  • 2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • 2¼ cup buttermilk — from the half-gallon on hand plus more
  • 2 large egg — lightly beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter — melted and cooled

Steps

  1. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together buttermilk, eggs, and vanilla extract.
  3. Slowly drizzle the melted butter into the wet ingredients while whisking.
  4. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir with a wooden spoon until just combined -- don't overmix.
  5. Heat a lightly greased skillet or griddle over medium-low heat.
  6. Scoop about 1/2 to 2/3 cup batter per pancake. Cook until the edges look set and bubbles burst, then flip and cook a few more minutes until golden.
  7. Serve warm with butter and maple syrup.

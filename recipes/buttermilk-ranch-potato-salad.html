Buttermilk Ranch Potato Salad

Serves 8 · 30 min total

americanvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 3 lb red potatoes — baby, halved or quartered
  • 6 tbsp cilantro — fresh, chopped
  • 6 tbsp dill — fresh, chopped
  • 6 tbsp parsley — fresh, chopped
  • ⅔ cup buttermilk — from the half-gallon on hand
  • ½ cup greek yogurt — plain
  • 6 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 6 tbsp scallions — chopped
  • 3 clove garlic — minced
  • 6 tbsp chives — fresh, chopped

Steps

  1. Boil the potatoes for 15-20 minutes, until tender. Drain and place in an ice bath to stop the cooking.
  2. Chop the cilantro, dill, parsley, and chives.
  3. Mix the herbs, buttermilk, Greek yogurt, mayonnaise, scallions, and garlic together.
  4. Mix in the cooled potatoes. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Garnish with extra herbs and serve.

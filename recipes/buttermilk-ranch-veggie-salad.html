Buttermilk Ranch Veggie Salad

Serves 24 · 25 min total

no-cookvegetarianpotluckkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 18 cup romaine lettuce — chopped, about 3 heads
  • 1½ ea red onion — sliced
  • 6 ea bell pepper — any color, sliced or diced
  • 3 medium cucumber — sliced or diced
  • 6 cup grape tomatoes — halved
  • 6 cup buttermilk — for dressing; from the half-gallon on hand plus more
  • 3 cup greek yogurt
  • 1½ cup mayonnaise
  • ¾ cup olive oil
  • 12 clove garlic — minced
  • 3 tsp salt — pantry staple
  • 1½ tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 6 tbsp dill — chopped, fresh
  • 6 tbsp chives — chopped, fresh
  • 6 tbsp parsley — chopped, fresh
  • 6 tsp onion powder
  • 6 tsp lemon juice

Steps

  1. Adapted from a grilled chicken salad recipe: dropped the chicken and avocado to make this a lettuce-and-veggie potluck side.
  2. Chop the romaine, slice the red onion and cucumber, and dice the bell peppers. Halve the tomatoes.
  3. Toss the romaine and vegetables together in a large bowl (or two, for easier transport).
  4. For the dressing: combine the buttermilk, Greek yogurt, mayonnaise, olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, dill, chives, parsley, onion powder, and lemon juice in a jar and shake well.
  5. Set aside a portion of the plain, undressed veggies for the toddlers at the potluck.
  6. Keep the dressing in a separate chilled container and transport the salad and dressing separately in a cooler. Toss together on arrival or shortly before serving -- it's a 30-minute drive and it'll be over 100F, so don't dress it ahead.

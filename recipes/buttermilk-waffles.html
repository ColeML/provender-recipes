Classic Buttermilk Waffles

Serves 8 · 40 min total

breakfast-for-dinnerwaffleskid-friendlyno-ovenmain

Ingredients

  • 2 ea eggs — large
  • 1¾ cup buttermilk
  • 8 tbsp unsalted butter — melted and cooled
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1¾ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup maple syrup — for serving

Steps

  1. In a medium mixing bowl, beat together the eggs, buttermilk, melted and cooled butter, and vanilla.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients, stirring just until nearly smooth. A few small lumps are fine - overmixing makes the waffles tough.
  4. Spray the waffle iron with non-stick cooking spray before preheating it.
  5. Cook the waffles according to the iron's directions. For an 8-inch round iron, use about 1/3 cup batter and cook 2-3 minutes, until the iron stops steaming.
  6. Serve with maple syrup. One batch makes about 10 waffles - enough for dinner with a couple left over. Leftovers reheat well in a toaster.

Chicken Burrito Bowls

Serves 8 · 50 min total

mexicanchickengriddlebuild-your-own

Ingredients

  • 3 cup jasmine rice — uncooked, about 8 cups cooked
  • 2½ lb chicken breast — griddled cilantro-lime, sliced
  • 1½ Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp taco seasoning — or fajita seasoning
  • 2 ea lime — juiced, for the chicken
  • 1 bunch fresh cilantro — chopped
  • 2 can black beans — 15 oz each, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cup frozen corn
  • 8 oz shredded cheese — about 2 cups
  • 24 oz salsa — about 1.5 jars
  • 12 oz sour cream
  • 2 ea avocado — for guacamole
  • 3 ea green onions — sliced

Steps

  1. Cook the rice (rice cooker).
  2. Toss the chicken with olive oil, taco/fajita seasoning, and lime juice; griddle outside until cooked through (165F). Rest and slice.
  3. Warm the black beans; thaw and warm the corn. Shred the cheese, slice the green onions, chop the cilantro, and mash the avocados into guacamole.
  4. Set out a build-your-own bar: rice, chicken, beans, corn, cheese, salsa, sour cream, guacamole, cilantro, green onions, and lime wedges.
  5. Note: family dinner for 4 with next-day leftover lunches. Chicken on the outdoor griddle to keep the kitchen cool. No oven.

Chicken Caesar Wraps (no-cook)

Serves 8 · 15 min total

caesarno-cookquickchickenkid-friendlymainno-oven

Ingredients

  • 1 ea rotisserie chicken — meat pulled and shredded, about 3 cups
  • 4 cup romaine lettuce — chopped, about 2 hearts
  • 1 cup grape tomatoes — halved
  • 1 cup parmesan — grated or shredded
  • ⅔ cup caesar dressing
  • 8 ea flour tortillas — large, burrito size
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Steps

  1. Pull the meat off a store rotisserie chicken and shred it into bite-size pieces (about 3 cups). Discard the skin and bones.
  2. Wash, drain, and chop the romaine. Cut the grape tomatoes in half.
  3. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, romaine, tomatoes, Parmesan, and Caesar dressing. Season with black pepper and toss until everything is evenly coated.
  4. Divide the mixture among the 8 large flour tortillas. Fold the bottom up, fold in the sides, then roll to the top like a burrito.
  5. Slice each wrap in half and serve. Nothing is cooked - the whole meal is assembly, which is the point on a hot day.
  6. Note: adapted from the Budget Bytes original, which pan-cooks raw chicken and toasts buttered breadcrumbs. Both steps are dropped here so the stove never turns on.

Chicken Fried Rice

Serves 8 · 35 min total

asianquickone-skilletkid-friendlymain

Ingredients

  • 4 clove garlic — minced
  • 2 tsp fresh ginger — grated
  • 6 ea green onions — sliced, whites and greens separated
  • 2 ea carrot — diced small
  • 2 ea red bell pepper — diced
  • 2 cup frozen peas
  • 1½ lb chicken breast — boneless skinless, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 4 ea eggs — large, lightly whisked
  • 6 tbsp cooking oil — divided
  • 4 tbsp toasted sesame oil — strong flavor - scaled sub-linearly; add more to taste
  • 6 tbsp soy sauce — to taste
  • 6 cup cooked rice — cooked and cooled (about 2 cups dry); make in the rice cooker earlier in the day

Steps

  1. Make the rice ahead of time (about 2 cups dry in the rice cooker) and let it cool completely — day-old or morning-made rice fries best.
  2. Prepare the vegetables: mince the garlic, grate the ginger, dice the carrots and bell peppers, slice the green onions (separate the green ends from the white ends), and measure the frozen peas.
  3. On a separate, clean cutting board, cut the chicken into 1/2 inch pieces so you get a little in every bite. Set aside (and wash your hands!).
  4. Lightly whisk the eggs in a small bowl. Heat a large skillet or the griddle over medium heat. Once hot, add 3 Tbsp of cooking oil and swirl to coat. Add the eggs and gently scramble until cooked through. Transfer the cooked eggs to a clean bowl or plate off to the side.
  5. Turn the heat up to medium-high. Add the diced carrots, stir, and cook for about two minutes. Next, add the bell peppers and the white firm ends of the green onions. Cook and stir for one minute more. Finally, add the frozen peas and stir and cook until heated through. Transfer the vegetables to a clean bowl or plate.
  6. Add the remaining cooking oil to the skillet and swirl to coat. Add the chicken pieces and cook through, about 4 minutes. Once the chicken is about half cooked (2-3 minutes), add the garlic and ginger and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes.
  7. Add the cooked and cooled rice to the skillet. Stir and cook the chicken and rice together for about 2 minutes, or until the rice is heated through. Pour the soy sauce and toasted sesame oil over the rice and stir until evenly combined. Turn the heat off.
  8. Add the eggs and vegetables back to the skillet and stir to combine, letting residual heat warm everything through. Top with the green ends of the sliced green onions. Taste and adjust soy sauce or sesame oil to your liking.

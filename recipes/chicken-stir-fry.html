Chicken Stir Fry

Serves 8 · 35 min total

asianstovetop

Ingredients

  • 7 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2½ tsp sesame oil — toasted
  • 3 clove garlic — minced
  • 2½ tsp ginger — grated fresh
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1⅓ tsp sriracha
  • 1 lb broccoli
  • 3 ea carrot
  • 1½ ea red bell pepper
  • 1½ small onion
  • 3 ea green onion
  • 2 lb chicken breast — boneless, skinless
  • 4 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

  1. Combine the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, sriracha, cornstarch, and water in a bowl for the stir fry sauce.
  2. Chop the broccoli into florets; slice the bell pepper, onion, and carrots; slice the green onions.
  3. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
  4. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok, brown the chicken, then remove to a plate.
  5. Add more oil, then stir-fry the carrots and broccoli for a minute, then the bell pepper and onion for another 1-2 minutes.
  6. Return the chicken to the skillet, pour in the sauce, and cook until it thickens and turns glossy.
  7. Top with green onions and serve.

Cilantro Lime Rice

Serves 6 · 30 min total

sidericeno-oven

Ingredients

  • 2 cup long grain white rice — uncooked; rinsed
  • 3 cup chicken broth — or water
  • ½ bunch cilantro — finely chopped (about 1 cup)
  • 1 whole lime — zest and juice
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice and drain well. Add rice and broth to a pot (or rice cooker), cover, and bring to a full boil without stirring.
  2. Reduce heat to low and simmer, covered, 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and rest, covered, 5 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, stir together the lime juice, 1/2 tsp lime zest, chopped cilantro, and olive oil.
  4. Fluff the rice with a fork, gently fold in the cilantro-lime mixture, and season with salt to taste.

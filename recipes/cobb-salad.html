Cobb Salad

Serves 8 · 40 min total

americanno-oven

Ingredients

  • 8 strip bacon — cooked and chopped
  • 2 ea chicken breast — cooked and diced
  • 2 head romaine lettuce — chopped
  • 4 ea roma tomato — chopped
  • 4 large egg — hard-boiled
  • 1 ea avocado
  • 4 ea green onion
  • 4 tbsp blue cheese — crumbled
  • ¼ cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 clove garlic — minced
  • ½ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • ½ tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ cup olive oil — pantry staple

Steps

  1. Cook the chicken breasts in the Instant Pot with a cup of water on high pressure for 10 minutes, quick release, then dice (adapted from the source recipe's oven method to keep the kitchen cool on the hottest day of the week).
  2. Microwave or pan-fry the bacon instead of baking it, for the same reason, then chop.
  3. Hard-boil the eggs on the stovetop, cool, peel, and slice.
  4. To make the dressing, whisk together the red wine vinegar, Dijon, oregano, garlic, salt, pepper, and sugar, then slowly stream in the olive oil while whisking until emulsified.
  5. Chop the romaine and tomatoes, slice the avocado and green onions.
  6. Arrange the romaine on a platter or in bowls, then top with rows of chicken, bacon, tomatoes, eggs, avocado, green onions, and blue cheese. Drizzle with dressing just before serving -- kids can pick around toppings they don't want.

Cold Peanut Noodle Salad

Serves 8 · 30 min total

asianvegetarianno-ovenno-cookmake-aheadleftover-friendly

Ingredients

  • 6 Tbsp peanut butter — natural-style, for the dressing
  • 2 Tbsp brown sugar — for the dressing
  • 2 clove garlic — minced, for the dressing
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger — grated, for the dressing
  • 2 whole lime — juiced, about 2 Tbsp juice
  • 4 tsp soy sauce — for the dressing
  • ½ cup neutral oil — canola or vegetable, for the dressing
  • 16 oz whole wheat spaghetti — broken in half before boiling
  • 8 cup red cabbage — finely shredded
  • 4 whole carrot — shredded on a large-holed grater
  • 1 bunch cilantro — roughly chopped, about 1/2 cup
  • 8 whole green onion — sliced, about 1/2 cup
  • 1 cup peanuts — roughly chopped

Steps

  1. Make the peanut lime dressing: combine the peanut butter, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, lime juice, soy sauce, and oil in a bowl or blender. Whisk or blend until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil. Break the spaghetti in half so it tosses into the salad more easily, then boil until tender. Drain, rinse briefly with cold water to cool, and let it drain well.
  3. Meanwhile, shred the cabbage as finely as possible with a knife or a food processor shredding attachment. Shred the carrots on a large-holed grater. Roughly chop the cilantro and peanuts, and slice the green onion.
  4. Combine the cooled, well-drained pasta in a large bowl with the cabbage, carrots, cilantro, green onion, and peanuts. Toss until evenly combined.
  5. Serve with the dressing right away, or keep the salad and dressing in separate containers and dress just before eating. Keeps 4-5 days refrigerated.

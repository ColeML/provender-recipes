Corn and Black Bean Salad

Serves 8 · 80 min total

tex-mexno-cookvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 6 cup corn kernels — fresh or frozen
  • 2 cup black beans — canned, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cup grape tomatoes — halved
  • 1 ea green bell pepper — diced
  • 1 ea red bell pepper — diced
  • ⅔ cup red onion — diced
  • 6 tbsp cilantro — chopped
  • 1 cup cotija cheese — or feta, optional
  • 6 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp lime juice — fresh, about 2 limes
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin

Steps

  1. Whisk the olive oil, lime juice, chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper in a small bowl for the dressing.
  2. Combine the corn, black beans, tomatoes, bell peppers, and onion in a large bowl.
  3. Pour the dressing over the salad, toss to combine, and season to taste.
  4. Chill for at least 1 hour, then top with cheese and cilantro before serving.

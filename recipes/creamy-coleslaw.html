Easy Creamy Coleslaw

Serves 8 · 15 min total

Americanno-cookvegetarianside

Ingredients

  • ½ head green cabbage — about 12 oz, finely shredded
  • 1 ea carrot — large, shredded
  • 6 ea green onions — sliced
  • ⅔ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp red wine vinegar — or apple cider vinegar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper — freshly cracked

Steps

  1. Finely shred the cabbage and carrot, then slice the green onions.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, honey, Dijon, red wine vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  3. Add the shredded cabbage, carrot, and green onions to a large bowl, pour the dressing over top, and stir until evenly coated. Serve immediately or chill until ready.

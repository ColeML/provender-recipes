Crock Pot Queso

Serves 16 · 110 min total

sidedippotluckslow-cookerno-ovenkid-friendlymexicanmake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 32 oz Velveeta — one 2 lb block, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 10 oz diced tomatoes and green chiles — one can Rotel, drained well
  • ⅔ cup whole milk — plus a splash more to thin as it sits
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ cup pickled jalapenos — chopped, optional - leave out or serve on the side for the kids
  • ¼ cup cilantro — chopped, for garnish
  • 2 bag tortilla chips — for serving

Steps

  1. Cut the Velveeta into 1-inch cubes. Smaller cubes melt evenly; a whole block scorches at the edges before the center gives up.
  2. Add the cubed cheese, drained Rotel, milk, cumin, and garlic powder to a small (2-quart) slow cooker. Stir once to distribute.
  3. Cover and cook on LOW for 1.5 to 2 hours, stirring every 30 minutes, until completely smooth. Do not use HIGH - the bottom scorches before the middle melts.
  4. Stir in the pickled jalapenos if you want heat. Thin with a splash more milk if it is thicker than you like.
  5. Switch to WARM to hold for serving. Stir every 30 minutes; it thickens as it sits and a splash of milk brings it back.
  6. Scatter the cilantro over the top just before serving. Serve with tortilla chips.
  7. Traveling with it: do NOT drive with melted queso in an unlocked crock pot. Cube the cheese at home, bag it dry with the drained Rotel and spices, and melt it on site - it only needs 90 minutes on LOW and the crock pot rides empty.
  8. If your small crock pot is 1.5 quarts rather than 2, drop to 24 oz of Velveeta and 2/3 of a can of Rotel so it is not filled to the rim.

Cucumber Tomato Salad

Serves 6 · 20 min total

Mediterraneanno-cookvegetarianside

Ingredients

  • 2 ea cucumbers — peeled and chopped
  • 4 ea Roma tomatoes — chopped
  • ½ ea red onion — small, sliced thin
  • 1 ea green bell pepper — chopped
  • ¼ bunch fresh parsley — chopped
  • 2 oz feta cheese — crumbled
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper — freshly cracked

Steps

  1. Whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper in a small bowl. Set aside.
  2. Peel the cucumbers if desired and dice the cucumbers, tomatoes, and bell pepper into chunks.
  3. Thinly slice the red onion and soak in ice water for 5-10 minutes, then drain.
  4. Chop the parsley and crumble the feta.
  5. Combine the cucumbers, tomatoes, bell pepper, red onion, parsley, and feta in a large bowl.
  6. Pour the dressing over and toss to combine. Serve immediately or refrigerate; stir before serving.

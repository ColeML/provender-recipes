Cucumber Tomato Salad

Serves 8 · 20 min total

mediterraneanno-cookvegetarian

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • 2½ tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1½ tbsp lemon juice
  • 1½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¾ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • ½ tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 3 ea cucumber — peeled and chopped
  • 5 ea roma tomato — chopped
  • 1 small red onion — thinly sliced
  • 1 large green bell pepper — chopped
  • ⅓ bunch parsley — chopped
  • 3 oz feta cheese — crumbled

Steps

  1. Whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper. Set the dressing aside.
  2. Dice the cucumbers, tomatoes, and bell pepper.
  3. Thinly slice the red onion and soak in ice water for 5-10 minutes, then drain.
  4. Pull and chop the parsley leaves; crumble the feta.
  5. Combine the cucumbers, tomatoes, bell pepper, red onion, parsley, and feta in a large bowl. Pour the dressing over top and toss.
  6. Serve immediately or refrigerate.

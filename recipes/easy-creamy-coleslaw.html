Easy Creamy Coleslaw

Serves 5 · 15 min total

americanno-cook

Ingredients

  • 7 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tsp honey
  • 1¼ tsp dijon mustard
  • 1¼ tsp red wine vinegar
  • ¼ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • ⅛ tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • ⅓ head green cabbage
  • 1 ea carrot — large
  • 4 ea green onion

Steps

  1. Finely shred the cabbage and carrot, then slice the green onions.
  2. Whisk together the mayonnaise, honey, Dijon, vinegar, salt, and pepper for the dressing.
  3. Combine the cabbage, carrot, and green onions in a large bowl, pour the dressing over top, and toss to coat.

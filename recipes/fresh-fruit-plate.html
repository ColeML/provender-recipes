Fresh Fruit Plate

Serves 6 · 10 min total

sideno-cooksummerkid-friendlyvegetarian

Ingredients

  • ¼ small watermelon — cubed, about 3 cups
  • 1 lb red grapes — rinsed, off the stem

Steps

  1. Cube the watermelon into 1-inch pieces.
  2. Rinse the grapes and pull them from the stems.
  3. Arrange on a platter and chill until serving.

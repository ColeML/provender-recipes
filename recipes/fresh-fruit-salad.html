Fresh Fruit Salad

Serves 5 · 20 min total

no-cookvegetarianbreakfast

Ingredients

  • ½ lb red grapes
  • 1 cup pineapple — chopped fresh
  • ½ lb strawberries — sliced
  • 3 oz blueberries
  • 3 oz blackberries
  • 2 ea kiwi — peeled and sliced
  • ½ ea lime — zested and juiced
  • 1 tbsp honey

Steps

  1. Wash and prep the fruit: slice the strawberries, peel and chop the kiwi.
  2. Combine all the fruit in a large bowl.
  3. Whisk together the lime zest, lime juice, and honey, then pour over the fruit and toss gently.

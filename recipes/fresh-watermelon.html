Fresh Watermelon

Serves 8 · 10 min total

sideno-cooksummerkid-friendlyvegetarian

Ingredients

  • ½ large watermelon — cut into wedges or cubes, about 6 cups

Steps

  1. Halve the watermelon and cut into wedges or 1-inch cubes, whichever the table prefers.
  2. Chill until serving. Keeps 3 days covered in the refrigerator.

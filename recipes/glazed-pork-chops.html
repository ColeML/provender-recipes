Glazed Pork Chops

Serves 8 · 40 min total

mainskilletquickkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¾ tsp cayenne powder — scaled sub-linearly for the kids; use 1 tsp for full heat
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper — freshly cracked
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 ea pork chops — thick cut boneless, about 4 lb; cook in two skillet batches

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350F (only needed if your chops are over one inch thick). In a small bowl, combine the brown sugar, cayenne, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Rub the seasoning mixture over all sides of each chop. The moisture from the meat helps the seasoning adhere.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium heat. When the oil shimmers, add the pork chops and cook about 5 minutes on each side, until nicely browned. With 8 chops, work in two batches so you don't crowd the pan.
  4. If your chops are more than one inch thick, transfer the skillet to the oven for another 5-10 minutes to finish. Check with a meat thermometer for an internal temperature of 145F.
  5. Dredge the chops through the thick sauce in the bottom of the pan just before serving.

Greek Marinated Chicken (griddle)

Serves 8 · 60 min total

greekgriddlemainno-oven

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 clove garlic — minced
  • ½ tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 ea lemon — zested, plus juice of half
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper — freshly cracked
  • ¼ bunch fresh parsley — roughly chopped
  • 4 lb chicken breast — boneless skinless breasts or thighs, for the griddle

Steps

  1. To make the marinade, combine the yogurt, olive oil, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and some freshly cracked pepper in a bowl.
  2. Zest the lemon into the bowl and add the juice from half of the lemon (about 1-2 Tbsp). Stir until well combined.
  3. Roughly chop about 1/4 bunch of parsley and stir it into the marinade.
  4. Add the chicken and marinade to a gallon zip-top bag. Remove as much air as possible, close tightly, and massage to coat the chicken well. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  5. Preheat the griddle over medium heat. Scrape excess marinade off the chicken and cook 5-7 minutes per side, until well browned and the internal temperature reaches 165F. (Original recipe bakes bone-in pieces at 375F for 45-60 minutes — use that if you'd rather not griddle.)
  6. Rest 5 minutes, then slice and serve.

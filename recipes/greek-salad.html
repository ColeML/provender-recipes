Greek Salad

Serves 8 · 15 min total

sidegreeksaladno-cook

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup red wine vinegar — for the dressing
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper — freshly cracked
  • 1 tsp dried oregano — recipe calls for 2 tsp fresh; dried works
  • 2 tbsp olive brine — from the kalamata olive jar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 clove garlic — minced
  • ½ cup olive oil — streamed in slowly
  • 3 head romaine lettuce — rinsed and chopped bite-size
  • 3 oz feta cheese — crumbled, divided (1 Tbsp goes in the dressing)
  • ⅓ cup kalamata olives — pitted, chopped
  • 1 cup grape tomatoes — halved
  • 1 ea cucumber — peeled and diced
  • ½ ea red onion — sliced thin

Steps

  1. Whisk the dressing ingredients together (red wine vinegar, honey, salt, pepper, oregano, olive brine, Dijon, 1 Tbsp crumbled feta, and minced garlic), streaming the olive oil in slowly. Set aside.
  2. Rinse and chop the romaine, crumble the feta, chop the olives, halve the tomatoes, peel and dice the cucumber, and thinly slice the red onion.
  3. Toss everything together with a drizzle of the dressing until dressed to your liking. Leftover dressing keeps refrigerated up to one week.

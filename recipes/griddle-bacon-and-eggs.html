Griddle Bacon and Eggs

Serves 8 · 30 min total

americanbreakfast-for-dinnerkid-friendlygluten-free

Ingredients

  • 32 strip bacon
  • 8 large egg
  • 1 pinch salt — pantry staple
  • 1 pinch black pepper — pantry staple

Steps

  1. Heat the griddle to 425F, or a griddle pan/skillet over medium-high heat on the stove.
  2. Place the bacon on the griddle in a single layer. Cook 4-5 minutes, flip, and cook 1-2 more minutes to desired crispiness.
  3. Drain the bacon on a paper-towel-lined plate.
  4. Crack the eggs into a bowl, season with salt and pepper, and whisk vigorously.
  5. Pour the eggs onto the griddle in the bacon grease and cook 3-4 minutes, flipping periodically with a spatula until scrambled through.

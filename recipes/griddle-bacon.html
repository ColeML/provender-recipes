Griddle Bacon

Serves 8 · 14 min total

sidebreakfastgriddleno-ovenkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 1 lb bacon

Steps

  1. Heat the griddle or a large skillet over medium.
  2. Lay the bacon out flat in a single layer. Cook 5-6 minutes per side, until as crisp as you like it.
  3. Move the bacon to a paper-towel-lined plate to drain, then serve alongside the waffles.

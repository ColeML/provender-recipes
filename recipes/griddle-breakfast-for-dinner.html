Griddle Breakfast for Dinner

Serves 6 · 35 min total

breakfast-for-dinnergriddleblackstoneno-ovenkid-friendlybudget

Ingredients

  • 1½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder — held just under a straight 1.5x scale - leavening does not scale linearly
  • 3 Tbsp granulated sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1⅛ cup milk — warmed
  • 3 Tbsp butter — melted, for the batter
  • ¾ tsp vanilla extract
  • 12 large eggs — 2 go in the pancake batter, 10 cook on the griddle
  • 12 oz bacon
  • 2 Tbsp cooking oil — for the griddle
  • 12 oz maple syrup — warmed, for serving
  • 4 Tbsp butter — softened, for serving

Steps

  1. Preheat the griddle to medium, about 350F. Outside on the Blackstone, give it a full 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. In a second bowl, whisk the milk, 2 of the eggs, the melted butter, and the vanilla. Pour into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined - lumps are fine and overmixing makes tough pancakes. Let the batter rest 5 minutes.
  4. Lay the bacon on one side of the griddle. Cook 8 to 10 minutes, turning once, until crisp. Move to a paper-towel-lined plate and wipe up the excess grease, leaving a thin film behind.
  5. Lightly oil the griddle. Pour the batter in 1/3-cup pools. Cook 2 to 3 minutes until bubbles form on top and the edges look set, then flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes more. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  6. Crack the remaining 10 eggs onto the cleared side of the griddle and cook them to order, fried or scrambled. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve family-style with butter and warm maple syrup.

Griddle Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowls

Serves 4 · 25 min total

asianseafoodrice-bowlno-oven

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb salmon filet — cut into 4 portions
  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • ½ Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 inch fresh ginger — grated
  • 1 clove garlic — minced
  • 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp rice wine — or rice vinegar (use a little less)
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • ¼ cup mayonnaise — for the sriracha mayo
  • 2 Tbsp sriracha — for the sriracha mayo
  • 2 cup long grain white rice — uncooked, for the bowls

Steps

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, 2 Tbsp water, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, rice wine, and cornstarch into a marinade.
  2. Cut the salmon into 4 portions, place in a zip-top bag with the marinade, and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
  3. Cook the rice (rice cooker or stovetop).
  4. Heat a well-oiled skillet or griddle over medium-high. Cook the salmon on all sides, 2-3 minutes each, until it flakes and reaches 145F. The sauce gets thick and sticky.
  5. Stir the mayonnaise with the sriracha. Serve the salmon over rice with a dollop of sriracha mayo.

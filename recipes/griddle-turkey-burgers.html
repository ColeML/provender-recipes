Griddle Turkey Burgers

Serves 8 · 22 min total

americangriddlekid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground turkey
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 6 tbsp parmesan cheese — shredded
  • 6 tbsp panko breadcrumbs
  • 8 ea hamburger buns

Steps

  1. Adapted from a grill recipe to the griddle, since there's no outdoor grill on hand.
  2. Combine the ground turkey, soy sauce, Parmesan, and panko in a bowl. Form into 8 patties.
  3. Heat the griddle to medium-high. Cook the patties about 5-6 minutes per side, until they reach 165F internally.
  4. Serve on toasted buns.

Grilled Cheese

Serves 4 · 15 min total

quickvegetariancomfortkid-friendlystovetop

Ingredients

  • 8 slice sandwich bread
  • 8 slice cheddar cheese — sliced
  • 4 Tbsp butter — softened, for the bread

Steps

  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Set a skillet or the griddle over medium heat. Place a slice butter-side-down, add 2 slices of cheese, then top with a second slice butter-side-up.
  3. Cook until golden, about 3-4 minutes, then flip and cook until the second side is golden and the cheese has melted, 2-3 minutes more.
  4. Repeat with the remaining bread and cheese. Slice in half and serve hot with the tomato soup.

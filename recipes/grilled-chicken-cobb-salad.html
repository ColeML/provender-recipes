Grilled Chicken Cobb Salad

Serves 4 · 25 min total

saladchickenno-ovensummer

Ingredients

  • 4 strip bacon — cooked and chopped
  • 1 lb chicken breast — griddled and diced
  • 1 head romaine lettuce — chopped
  • 2 ea roma tomatoes — chopped
  • 2 ea hard-boiled eggs
  • 1 ea avocado
  • 2 ea green onions — sliced
  • 2 Tbsp blue cheese — crumbled
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1 clove garlic — minced
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • ¼ cup olive oil

Steps

  1. Griddle the chicken outside (season with salt and pepper), rest, then dice. Cook the bacon on the stovetop until crisp and chop.
  2. Whisk the dressing: red wine vinegar, Dijon, oregano, garlic, salt, pepper, and sugar. Slowly stream in the olive oil while whisking.
  3. Make a bed of chopped romaine. Arrange the chicken, bacon, tomatoes, eggs, avocado, green onions, and blue cheese in rows on top.
  4. Dress just before serving.
  5. Note: no oven - chicken on the outdoor griddle, bacon on the stovetop. Made fresh for 4; eat day-of (greens do not keep for lunch).

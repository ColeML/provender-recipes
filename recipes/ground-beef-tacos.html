Stovetop Ground Beef Tacos

Serves 8 · 25 min total

mexicanbeefquickkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground beef
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup water
  • 16 ea flour tortillas — taco size
  • 8 oz cheddar cheese — shredded
  • 1 ea lettuce — head, shredded
  • 2 ea roma tomato — diced
  • 1 ea yellow onion — diced
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • ¼ cup cilantro — chopped
  • 1 ea lime — cut into wedges

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until cooked through. Drain excess fat.
  2. Stir in the chili powder, smoked paprika, cumin, cayenne, oregano, salt, pepper, and water. Simmer 3-5 minutes until thickened.
  3. Warm the tortillas on a hot griddle about 30 seconds per side.
  4. Assemble tacos with the beef, cheddar, lettuce, tomato, onion, cilantro, a dollop of sour cream, and a squeeze of lime.

Hawaiian Macaroni Salad

Serves 8 · 25 min total

sidehawaiiancoldno-oven

Ingredients

  • 16 oz elbow macaroni — cooked soft
  • 2 whole carrot — peeled and grated
  • 1½ cup mayonnaise — creamy dressing base
  • 2 tsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ bunch green onions — sliced, to garnish
  • salt — to taste
  • black pepper — to taste

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the macaroni a few minutes past al dente so it is very soft. Drain and rinse with cold water.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the cooled macaroni with the grated carrot, mayonnaise, sugar, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Stir to combine, adjusting seasoning to taste. Serve garnished with green onions, or chill until ready.

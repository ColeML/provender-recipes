Homemade Cinnamon Rolls

Serves 27 · 130 min total

americanbreakfastdessertpotluckkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 1½ cup water — hot, 100-110F
  • 2 tbsp dry yeast — instant or bread machine yeast
  • 6 tbsp sugar — granulated
  • 6 tbsp butter — melted, for the dough
  • 1 cup whole milk — for the dough
  • 6 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1½ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • 18 tbsp butter — room temperature, for the filling
  • 1½ cup brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp cinnamon
  • ⅜ tsp salt — for the filling
  • 12 oz cream cheese — room temperature
  • 6 tbsp butter — room temperature, for the icing
  • 3 tbsp whole milk — for the icing
  • 1½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 4½ cup powdered sugar

Steps

  1. Tripled from the source recipe's single batch (9 rolls) for the potluck crowd -- work in batches or use multiple bowls/pans as needed.
  2. Stir together the hot water, yeast, and sugar; let stand about 5 minutes until foamy.
  3. Whisk in the milk and melted butter.
  4. Stir together 3 cups of the flour and the salt in a large bowl, then add the liquid and stir until smooth.
  5. Add the remaining flour gradually until a soft, slightly tacky dough forms. Knead about 3 minutes on a floured surface.
  6. Return the dough to the bowl, cover, and let rise 1 hour or until doubled.
  7. Punch down the dough, divide into 3 portions, and roll each into a 8x13-inch rectangle.
  8. Beat together the room-temperature filling butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt into a paste; spread over each rectangle.
  9. Roll each rectangle into a log and slice into 9 pieces. Place into greased baking dishes (3 dishes, 9 rolls each).
  10. Let rise 10-15 minutes while preheating the oven to 350F, then bake 30 minutes until golden brown.
  11. Beat together the cream cheese, icing butter, milk, and vanilla, then beat in the powdered sugar to make the icing.
  12. Bake Wednesday night; let cool, then ice and cover for transport to Thursday's potluck. A quick reheat (a few seconds in the microwave) before icing brings back the fresh-baked texture.

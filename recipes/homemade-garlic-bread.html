Homemade Garlic Bread

Serves 12 · 25 min total

sidebreadquick

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French bread — approx. 24 inches long
  • 6 tbsp butter — room temperature
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 clove garlic — minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley — chopped
  • ⅛ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400F. In a bowl, stir together the room-temperature butter, olive oil, minced garlic, garlic powder, chopped parsley, and salt until relatively smooth.
  2. Cut the loaf in half into two 12-inch pieces, then cut each piece open lengthwise. Lay the bread on a baking sheet cut sides up and spread the garlic butter evenly over the surfaces.
  3. Bake 10-15 minutes, until the edges are golden brown and crispy — less time for softer bread, more for crispier. Cut into 2-inch sections and serve hot.

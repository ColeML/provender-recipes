Honey Garlic Chicken (from frozen)

Serves 8 · 50 min total

asianchickeninstant-potone-potfrom-frozenno-ovenkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 4 ea chicken breast — boneless, skinless, about 2.75 lb - frozen, left whole
  • ⅔ cup honey
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp sriracha
  • 4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • ¼ cup water — half the thawed version - frozen chicken releases its own
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch — whisked into 3 Tbsp cold water at the end
  • 4 tbsp butter — 2 Tbsp to bloom the garlic, 2 Tbsp stirred in at the finish
  • 8 clove garlic — minced
  • 2 tsp salt — into the sauce, plus a little on the cut chicken
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 4 ea green onion — sliced, for the top

Steps

  1. Separate the frozen breasts. If they are frozen into one solid block, run cold water over them until they come apart - a block cooks unevenly and the center can miss temperature.
  2. Set the Instant Pot to Saute. Melt 2 Tbsp of the butter and cook the minced garlic 45 to 60 seconds until fragrant but not browned. This is where the flavor from the missing sear comes back - do not skip it.
  3. Press Cancel. Add the water, honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sriracha, sesame oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Stir and scrape the bottom clean so nothing triggers a burn warning.
  4. Lay the frozen breasts in the sauce in a single layer. Do not stack them.
  5. Lid on, valve sealed. High Pressure 12 minutes for average breasts, 15 minutes if they are over an inch thick. It will take 15 to 20 minutes to come to pressure from frozen - that is normal, not a problem.
  6. Natural release 5 minutes, then quick release the rest.
  7. Check the thickest breast with a thermometer: it must read 165F. With frozen meat the thermometer beats the timer. If it is under, put the lid back on and run 2 to 3 more minutes.
  8. Move the chicken to a board, cut into 1/2-inch pieces, and season lightly with salt and pepper - frozen meat cannot be seasoned before cooking, so it happens here.
  9. Set to Saute. Whisk the cornstarch into 3 Tbsp cold water and stir into the simmering sauce. Cook 2 to 3 minutes until it is glossy and coats a spoon. Frozen chicken sheds a lot of water, so if it is still loose add another slurry of 1 Tbsp cornstarch in 1 Tbsp cold water.
  10. Cancel the heat. Stir in the remaining 2 Tbsp butter and the chicken. Scatter the green onions over the top.
  11. How this differs from the thawed version: no flour dredge and no sear, because neither is possible on frozen meat. The water is cut from 1/2 cup to 1/4 cup and the cornstarch raised from 2 to 3 Tbsp to offset the water the chicken releases. Blooming the garlic in butter up front recovers some of the flavor the sear would have contributed.

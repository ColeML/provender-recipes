Honey Garlic Chicken (Instant Pot)

Serves 8 · 35 min total

asianchickeninstant-potone-potkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • ⅔ cup honey
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp sriracha
  • 4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch — mixed into a slurry at the end
  • ½ cup water — for pressure cooking
  • 4 ea chicken breast — boneless, skinless (about 2.75 lb), cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 6 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 8 clove garlic — minced
  • 4 ea green onion — sliced

Steps

  1. Make the sauce. Whisk together the honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sriracha, and toasted sesame oil in a small bowl. Set aside — hold the cornstarch for the end.
  2. Cut the chicken breasts into small 1/2-inch pieces. Season with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder, then toss in the flour until each piece is evenly coated.
  3. Set the Instant Pot to Saute (high). Add the olive oil and butter. Once hot, brown the chicken in batches until golden on all sides (it does not need to cook through). Remove to a plate between batches.
  4. Add the minced garlic and saute 30-60 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the water and scrape up all the browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Turn Saute off. (Scraping the fond loose is what prevents the burn warning.)
  5. Return the chicken to the pot. Pour the sauce evenly over the top and do NOT stir — keeping the honey off the bottom keeps it from scorching under pressure. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook on High for 4 minutes.
  6. Quick-release the pressure, open the lid, and stir. Set the Instant Pot back to Saute. Stir the cornstarch into 2 Tbsp cold water to make a slurry, pour it in, and simmer 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  7. Turn off the pot, top with sliced green onions, and serve over rice.

Instant Pot Beef and Broccoli

Serves 8 · 45 min total

asianbeefinstant-potno-oven

Ingredients

  • 4 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • 12 clove garlic — minced
  • 4 Tbsp fresh ginger — grated
  • 3 lb beef flank steak — sliced thin against the grain
  • 1 cup water — to deglaze
  • 1 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 4 tsp brown sugar — or honey
  • 4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 cup beef stock — or water
  • 4 Tbsp cornstarch — mixed with 4 Tbsp water
  • 3 lb broccoli florets — steamed
  • 4 tsp sesame seeds — to garnish
  • 2 cup jasmine rice — uncooked, to serve

Steps

  1. Set the Instant Pot to SAUTE. Heat the oil and saute the garlic and ginger 30 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Add the beef and brown on all sides (work in batches so it sears rather than steams). Deglaze with 1 cup water, scraping the bottom.
  3. Stir in the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, and beef stock. Turn off SAUTE.
  4. Lid on, vent to SEALING. PRESSURE COOK 8 minutes on high. Quick-release the steam.
  5. Set to SAUTE again; stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook 1-2 minutes until glossy. Add a little more slurry if you want it thicker.
  6. Turn off and stir in the steamed broccoli. Serve over rice, garnish with sesame seeds.
  7. Note: 4x batch (8 servings). Cook the rice in the rice cooker alongside. No oven.

Instant Pot Kalua Pork with Cabbage

Serves 8 · 105 min total

hawaiianporkinstant-potno-ovenbig-batch

Ingredients

  • 4 lb pork shoulder — boneless pork butt / shoulder roast
  • 1 Tbsp coarse sea salt — Hawaiian Alaea salt if you have it, or any coarse sea salt
  • 1 Tbsp liquid smoke
  • 8 clove garlic — whole, peeled, lightly smashed
  • 1 cup chicken broth — or water
  • 1 whole cabbage — medium; cored and cut into 1-inch strips
  • 1 bunch green onions — thinly sliced, to garnish
  • 2 cup long grain white rice — to serve, uncooked
  • black pepper — to taste

Steps

  1. Cut the pork into 3-4 equal pieces. Cut small slits and tuck a garlic clove into each.
  2. Add the broth to the Instant Pot. Add the pork and pierce the tops a few times. Rub with the sea salt, then pour the liquid smoke over.
  3. Lock the lid and pressure-cook on Manual/High for 90 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally.
  4. Remove the pork to a tray and shred with two forks. Moisten with some of the cooking liquid and check the seasoning.
  5. Add the cut cabbage to the liquid in the pot, lock the lid, and pressure-cook on High 3 minutes with a quick release. Fold the cabbage into the shredded pork.
  6. Serve over cooked white rice, garnished with green onions.
  7. Great for leftovers - reheats well for lunches.

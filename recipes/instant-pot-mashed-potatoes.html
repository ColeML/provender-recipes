Instant Pot Mashed Potatoes

Serves 7 · 60 min total

sideinstant-potno-ovenkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 4 lb russet potatoes — peeled, large cubes
  • 6 cup water
  • 3 tsp salt — divided
  • ½ tsp black pepper — freshly cracked
  • 6 tbsp butter — softened
  • ½ cup milk — warmed

Steps

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into large cubes.
  2. Place them in the Instant Pot, cover with 6 cups of water, and sprinkle in 2 teaspoons of salt.
  3. Seal the valve, set to pressure cook for 12 minutes (it takes about 30 minutes to come to pressure first).
  4. After the timer goes off, turn off the Instant Pot and carefully quick-release the valve with a utensil until the steam subsides.
  5. Drain the potatoes in a colander, using oven mitts to handle the insert.
  6. Return the potatoes to the insert and add the remaining salt, the pepper, butter, and slowly add the warmed milk.
  7. Gently mash with a potato masher to your desired consistency. Don't over-mash or they'll get gummy.

Instant Pot Turkey Chili

Serves 8 · 33 min total

tex-mexinstant-potfreezer-friendly

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground turkey
  • 8 strip turkey bacon — chopped
  • 15 oz pinto beans — canned, drained
  • 15 oz black beans — canned, drained
  • 15 oz fire roasted tomatoes — canned, diced, drained
  • 6 oz tomato paste — canned
  • 1 small red onion — chopped
  • ½ ea red bell pepper — seeded and chopped
  • ½ ea orange bell pepper — seeded and chopped
  • 1 ea jalapeno — seeded and minced, optional
  • 2 cup chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp kosher salt — pantry staple
  • 1 tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp garlic — minced
  • ½ cup sour cream — for serving
  • ¼ cup cilantro — for serving
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese — shredded, for serving

Steps

  1. Turn the Instant Pot to saute and cook the turkey bacon until crisp, stirring often. Remove to a paper-towel-lined plate.
  2. Add the onion and peppers and cook until tender. Add the ground turkey and cook until browned, draining excess grease.
  3. Add the beans, tomatoes, tomato paste, chicken stock, spices, garlic, and Worcestershire sauce, plus 3/4 of the bacon. Stir to combine.
  4. Switch to pressure cook (chili setting or high pressure) for 18-20 minutes. Let pressure release naturally for 10-15 minutes, or quick release.
  5. Serve topped with lime, sour cream, cheese, and the reserved bacon.

Italian Grinder Sliders (potluck 3x batch)

Serves 18 · 35 min total

italianpotlucksandwichbig-batchmain

Ingredients

  • 36 ea Hawaiian rolls — three 12-packs
  • 24 oz deli ham — thinly sliced
  • 12 oz provolone cheese — sliced
  • 9 oz pepperoni — thinly sliced
  • 9 oz salami — thinly sliced
  • 6 tbsp butter — softened
  • 6 tbsp grated parmesan
  • 1½ tsp garlic powder — for the parmesan butter
  • ¾ tsp salt — for the parmesan butter
  • 24 oz shredded lettuce — for the grinder salad
  • 1½ cup tomato — diced, about 3 roma tomatoes
  • 1½ cup pepperoncini peppers — sliced, one 16 oz jar
  • ¾ cup mayonnaise — for the dressing
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar — for the dressing
  • 1 tbsp Italian seasoning — for the dressing
  • 1½ tsp salt — for the dressing
  • 1½ tsp black pepper — for the dressing
  • 1½ tsp garlic powder — for the dressing

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350F. This is a triple batch (36 sliders) — use three 9x13 baking dishes or two large sheet pans.
  2. Cut the Hawaiian rolls horizontally, keeping each 12-pack connected. Lay the bottoms in the baking dishes.
  3. Layer the provolone on the bread, then the ham, pepperoni, and salami on top. Place the tops back on the rolls and cut through between each roll.
  4. Combine the butter, parmesan, garlic powder, and salt. Spread over the tops of the sliders. Bake 15-20 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the tops are golden brown. Rotate pans halfway if the oven is crowded.
  5. While the sandwiches bake, make the grinder salad: whisk the mayonnaise, red wine vinegar, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and garlic powder into a dressing, then toss with the lettuce, tomatoes, and pepperoncini.
  6. Once the sliders are done, remove the tops, add the grinder salad, and put the tops back on. For the potluck, add the grinder salad just before serving so the rolls stay crisp.

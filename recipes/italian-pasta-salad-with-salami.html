Italian Pasta Salad with Salami

Serves 10 · 23 min total

italiancoldmake-aheadno-oven

Ingredients

  • 1 lb rotini pasta
  • 6 oz salami — diced, for protein
  • 1 whole english cucumber — sliced into half moons
  • 1 whole red bell pepper — chopped
  • 1 whole orange bell pepper — chopped
  • 10 oz grape tomatoes — halved
  • ½ whole red onion — thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp parsley — fresh, chopped
  • ¼ cup feta cheese — crumbled
  • 16 oz zesty Italian dressing — bottled
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package directions. Drain, rinse briefly with cool water, and drain well.
  2. While the pasta cooks, slice the cucumber into half moons, chop the bell peppers, halve the tomatoes, thinly slice the red onion, dice the salami, and chop the parsley.
  3. Combine the drained pasta with the vegetables, salami, parsley, and feta in a large bowl.
  4. Pour the Italian dressing over and toss until evenly coated. Season with salt and pepper. Serve, or chill until ready (keeps several days).

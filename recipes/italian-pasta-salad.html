Italian Pasta Salad

Serves 8 · 25 min total

italianpastasidemake-ahead

Ingredients

  • 1 lb rotini pasta
  • 1 ea english cucumber — sliced into half-moons
  • 1 ea red bell pepper — chopped
  • 1 ea orange bell pepper — chopped
  • 8 oz grape tomatoes — halved
  • ½ ea red onion — thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp fresh parsley — chopped
  • ¼ cup feta cheese — crumbled
  • 14 oz zesty Italian dressing — bottled
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta per package directions. Drain, rinse briefly with cool water, and drain well.
  2. Prep the veg: half-moon the cucumber, chop the peppers, halve the tomatoes, thinly slice the onion, and chop the parsley.
  3. In a large bowl combine the pasta, vegetables, parsley, and feta.
  4. Pour over the dressing and toss to coat. Season with salt and pepper. Serve, or refrigerate until ready.
  5. Make-ahead: holds well up to a day in the fridge. Add a splash more dressing before serving as the pasta soaks it up.

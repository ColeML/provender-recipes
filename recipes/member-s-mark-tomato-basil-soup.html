Member's Mark Tomato Basil Soup

Serves 4 · 5 min total

sidestore-boughtno-cooksoupvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 tub Member's Mark tomato basil soup — store-bought (Sam's Club), heat and serve

Steps

  1. Heat the store-bought tomato basil soup on the stovetop or in the microwave following the tub instructions.
  2. Ladle into bowls and serve alongside the grilled cheese.

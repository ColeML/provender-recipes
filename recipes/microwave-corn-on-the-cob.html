Microwave Corn on the Cob

Serves 6 · 28 min total

Americanquickno-ovenvegetarianside

Ingredients

  • 6 ea corn on the cob — in the husk
  • 6 tbsp salted butter

Steps

  1. Cut the cobs to fit your microwave, using its inner revolving plate as a guide. Leave the husks on.
  2. Run each cob under water to soak, then wrap in a wet paper towel. Microwave on high about 4 minutes per cob.
  3. The cobs are done when the kernels deepen in color and turn glossy. Let cool until you can handle them, then trim the stem end.
  4. Peel the husks from the top down, clean off any silk, top each cob with butter, and serve.

One Pot Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta

Serves 8 · 35 min total

cajunone-potquickmain

Ingredients

  • 4 tsp smoked paprika — for the Cajun seasoning
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper — keeps it mild for the kids; add more at the table
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 lb chicken breast — boneless skinless, cut into 1/2 to 3/4-inch cubes
  • 2 ea yellow onion — diced
  • 1 lb penne pasta — uncooked
  • 2 can fire roasted diced tomatoes — 15 oz cans, with juices
  • 4 cup chicken broth
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 6 ea green onions — sliced, for topping

Steps

  1. Combine the Cajun seasoning ingredients in a small bowl. Cut the chicken into 1/2 to 3/4-inch cubes, place in a bowl, pour the seasoning over top, and stir to coat.
  2. Add the olive oil and butter to a large deep skillet or pot. Heat over medium-high until the butter is melted and foamy. Add the seasoned chicken and cook a couple minutes on each side, just until the outside gets some color — it doesn't need to be cooked through yet.
  3. Add the diced yellow onion and saute about 2 minutes more, until it begins to soften, letting the onion's moisture dissolve the browned bits from the bottom.
  4. Add the pasta, fire roasted diced tomatoes (with juices), and chicken broth. Stir until evenly combined, then put a lid on and bring the broth to a boil.
  5. Once boiling, turn the heat down to medium-low and simmer about ten minutes, stirring every couple of minutes, until the pasta is tender and the liquid is thick and saucy. With the doubled batch, give it a couple extra minutes if needed.
  6. Add the cream cheese in chunks and stir until melted into the sauce. Top with sliced green onions and serve.

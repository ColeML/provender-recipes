Instant Pot Pulled Pork

Serves 8 · 100 min total

bbqporkinstant-potpressure-cookerkid-friendlyleftover-friendlysandwich

Ingredients

  • 3 lb pork butt — cut into large chunks
  • 1 yellow onion — cut into chunks
  • 4 cloves garlic — roughly chopped
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 bottle BBQ sauce — about 18 oz, for serving
  • 8 hamburger buns
  • 1 cup chicken broth — or water

Steps

  1. Combine the spice rub: paprika, smoked paprika, brown sugar, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Cut the pork butt into large chunks and toss with the spice rub until evenly coated.
  3. Optional: set the Instant Pot to Sauté and brown the pork chunks on a couple of sides for extra flavor, working in batches. Skip this to save time.
  4. Add the onion and garlic to the pot, set the pork on top, and pour in 1 cup chicken broth. Scrape up any browned bits so you don't trigger a burn warning.
  5. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook on High for 60 minutes.
  6. Let the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes, then quick-release any remaining pressure.
  7. Transfer the pork to a bowl, shred with two forks, and moisten with a little of the cooking liquid. Toss with BBQ sauce to taste.
  8. Pile onto buns and serve. For crispy edges, broil the shredded pork a few minutes first.

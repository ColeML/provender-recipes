Quickie Red Beans and Rice

Serves 8 · 60 min total

CajunCreoleone-potkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 18 oz Andouille sausage — or mild smoked sausage for the kids
  • 4 can kidney beans — 15 oz cans
  • 2 cup long grain white rice — uncooked
  • 1 ea yellow onion — diced
  • 1 ea bell pepper — diced
  • 4 rib celery — diced
  • 5 clove garlic — minced
  • 4 ea green onions — sliced
  • 1½ cup chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper — reduced for kids
  • ½ tsp black pepper — freshly cracked

Steps

  1. Slice the sausage into rounds. Add the sausage and cooking oil to a large pot and cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until browned.
  2. While the sausage cooks, dice the onion, bell pepper, and celery, and mince the garlic.
  3. Add the onion, bell pepper, celery, and garlic to the pot and cook until the onions are soft and translucent.
  4. Add the smoked paprika, oregano, thyme, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, and black pepper; stir for a minute more.
  5. Drain three cans of beans. Add the fourth can with its liquid to a blender and puree until smooth. Add the drained beans, pureed beans, and chicken broth to the pot; stir to combine.
  6. Cover and bring to a boil, then reduce to medium-low and simmer about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Meanwhile, cook the rice: add it to a pot with 4 cups water, cover, and bring to a boil; reduce to low and simmer 15 minutes, then rest covered 5 minutes and fluff.
  8. Taste the beans and add salt if needed. Serve over rice, topped with sliced green onions.

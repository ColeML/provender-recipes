Slow Cooker Sausage and Peppers Hoagies

Serves 8 · 380 min total

italiansausagesandwichslow-cooker

Ingredients

  • 13 link sweet Italian sausage — about 3.25 lb
  • 8 ea bell peppers — red/green/yellow mix, sliced
  • 3 ea onions — large, sliced
  • 6 clove garlic — minced
  • 16 oz marinara sauce — for slow-cooker moisture
  • 1½ Tbsp olive oil — to brown the sausage
  • 3 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • 2 tsp kosher salt — to taste
  • 12 ea hoagie rolls
  • 12 slice provolone
  • 1 ea grainy mustard or mayo — optional, for serving

Steps

  1. Slice the onions and peppers and mince the garlic; add them to the slow cooker.
  2. Recommended: brown the sausages in a skillet with the olive oil, a few minutes per side, then nestle them on top of the vegetables. (Or add them raw to save time.)
  3. Pour over the marinara, then add the balsamic, red pepper flakes, and salt.
  4. Cover and cook on LOW 6-7 hours or HIGH 3-4 hours, until the peppers are tender and the sausages reach 165F.
  5. Toast the hoagie rolls, lay on provolone, and pile a sausage or two per serving with peppers and onions. Spread with mustard or mayo if you like.
  6. Note: family dinner for 4 plus leftover lunches (~13 links). Slow cooker keeps the kitchen cool.

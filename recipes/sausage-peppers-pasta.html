Pasta with Sausage and Peppers

Serves 8 · 30 min total

italianporkquickone-pot

Ingredients

  • 4 link Italian sausage — about 1 lb
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 ea red bell pepper — sliced into thin strips
  • 2 ea yellow onion — sliced into thin strips
  • 2 jar pasta sauce — 24 oz each
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  • 1 lb penne pasta
  • 2 oz feta cheese — crumbled
  • 2 tbsp parsley — chopped (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium. Add the cooking oil and sausage links and cook a few minutes per side until well browned (not fully cooked yet).
  2. Remove the sausage to a cutting board and slice into medallions. Return to the skillet and cook until cooked through and browned on the cut sides. Remove to a plate.
  3. Add the sliced onion and bell pepper to the skillet. Cook over medium heat just until they begin to soften.
  4. Add the sausage back along with the pasta sauce, basil, oregano, and crushed red pepper. Stir to combine and heat through.
  5. Meanwhile, boil the penne until tender, about 7 minutes. Drain and add to the skillet, stirring to combine with the sauce.
  6. Top with crumbled feta and chopped parsley, then serve.

Sesame Cucumber Salad

Serves 6 · 15 min total

sideasianno-cook

Ingredients

  • 2 large cucumbers — peeled and sliced
  • ⅓ cup rice vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp granulated sugar
  • ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  • ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 3 whole green onions — sliced
  • ¼ cup peanuts — chopped

Steps

  1. Combine the rice vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, crushed red pepper, and salt to make the dressing.
  2. Peel and slice the cucumbers and place in a large bowl.
  3. Chop the peanuts and slice the green onions.
  4. Add the peanuts, green onions, and dressing to the cucumbers. Stir to combine. Serve, or chill until ready and stir again before serving.

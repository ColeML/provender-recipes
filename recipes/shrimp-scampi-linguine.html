Shrimp Scampi over Linguine

Serves 8 · 20 min total

seafoodshrimpskilletquickno-ovenmainitalian

Ingredients

  • 2 lb shrimp — peeled and deveined, tail off
  • 1 lb linguine
  • 1 tsp salt — divided
  • 1 tsp black pepper — freshly cracked, divided
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 8 clove garlic — minced
  • ½ tsp creole seasoning — such as Tony Chachere's
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 4 tbsp fresh parsley — chopped
  • 2 ea lemon — juiced, about 4 tbsp
  • ¼ tsp chili flakes — optional

Steps

  1. If the shrimp are frozen, put them in a colander and run cool water over them for about 5 minutes, until thawed.
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the linguine according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.
  3. Pat the shrimp dry on both sides with paper towels. Season with half the salt and half the black pepper.
  4. In a large 12-inch skillet, heat the olive oil and butter over medium. Once hot, add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the remaining salt and pepper, the Creole seasoning, and the chicken broth. Whisk together and simmer 2-3 minutes until the sauce reduces by about half.
  6. Add the shrimp and cook about 2 minutes on the first side, just until pink and opaque.
  7. Flip the shrimp, then stir in the parsley, lemon juice, and chili flakes. Cook about 2 more minutes. Do not overcook - shrimp turn rubbery fast.
  8. Take the skillet off the heat, toss the shrimp and sauce with the drained linguine, and serve with extra parsley and lemon.

Old-Fashioned Sloppy Joes

Serves 8 · 30 min total

Americanquickkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground beef
  • 2 ea yellow onion — finely diced
  • 2 ea green bell pepper — finely diced
  • 3 clove garlic — minced
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil
  • 2 can tomato sauce — 15 oz cans
  • 5 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1½ tsp chili powder
  • 1½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 ea hamburger buns — toasted

Steps

  1. Finely dice the onion and bell pepper, and mince the garlic.
  2. Add the olive oil and beef to a large skillet and cook over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, and bell pepper to the skillet. Saute until the onions are soft and translucent.
  4. Add the tomato sauce, tomato paste, vinegar, brown sugar, Dijon, chili powder, Worcestershire, and salt. Stir to combine.
  5. Simmer over medium-low for about 5-10 minutes until thickened to your liking.
  6. Serve over toasted buns.

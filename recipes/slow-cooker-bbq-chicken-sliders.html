Instant Pot BBQ Chicken Sliders

Serves 8 · 30 min total

americaninstant-potkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 2.6 lb chicken breast — boneless, skinless
  • ½ cup water — needed to bring the Instant Pot to pressure
  • ⅓ tsp garlic powder
  • ⅓ tsp onion powder
  • ⅔ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • ⅓ tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 8 oz bbq sauce
  • 16 ea slider buns
  • 2 cup coleslaw mix — for topping the sliders
  • ⅔ cup mayonnaise — for the coleslaw dressing
  • 1⅓ tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1⅓ tbsp dijon mustard
  • ⅔ tsp celery salt

Steps

  1. Add the chicken breasts, water, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper to the Instant Pot (no BBQ sauce yet) -- the water is needed to reach pressure.
  2. Lock the lid, close the pressure valve, and cook on high pressure for 15 minutes (20 if the chicken is frozen).
  3. Quick release the pressure. Remove the chicken to a cutting board and shred with two forks, then return it to the pot.
  4. Switch to saute/simmer, stir in the BBQ sauce, and simmer 5 minutes for the best flavor.
  5. Whisk together the mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, and celery salt in a small bowl; toss with the coleslaw mix.
  6. Slice the slider buns, pile on the BBQ chicken, top with a scoop of coleslaw, and add the top bun.

Slow Cooker BBQ Chicken Sliders

Serves 8 · 160 min total

americanslow-cookerkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 2.6 lb chicken breast — boneless, skinless
  • 4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt — pantry staple
  • 1 tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 36 oz bbq sauce
  • 16 ea slider buns

Steps

  1. Combine the smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
  2. Sprinkle the seasoning over both sides of the chicken breasts.
  3. Place the chicken in the slow cooker and pour the BBQ sauce over top, making sure the chicken is fully covered.
  4. Cook on high for 2.5 hours or low for 4-5 hours.
  5. Shred the chicken with two forks once it pulls apart easily.
  6. Pile onto slider buns and serve.

Street Corn Salad (Esquites)

Serves 8 · 25 min total

mexicansidecornmake-aheadno-oven

Ingredients

  • 4 cup frozen corn kernels — thawed and patted very dry
  • 4 Tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 Tbsp plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 ea lime — zested and juiced
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • ½ tsp chili powder
  • ½ tsp sea salt — to taste
  • ⅓ cup fresh cilantro — minced
  • 4 Tbsp cotija cheese — crumbled
  • 3 Tbsp pickled red onion
  • 1½ Tbsp pickled jalapeno

Steps

  1. Whisk the dressing: mayonnaise, Greek yogurt, lime zest and juice, smoked paprika, cayenne, chili powder, and salt.
  2. Thaw the frozen corn and pat it very dry (so it browns instead of steaming). In a very hot dry skillet, char the corn in batches, stirring every 30 seconds until browned in spots. Cool slightly.
  3. Toss the charred corn with the dressing, cotija, cilantro, pickled onion, and jalapeno.
  4. Serve warm or chilled.
  5. Make-ahead: char the corn and toss everything up to 1-2 days ahead and refrigerate. Stir in the fresh cilantro and a fresh squeeze of lime, and re-season, just before serving. No oven.

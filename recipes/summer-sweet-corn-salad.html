Summer Sweet Corn Salad

Serves 8 · 25 min total

sidesaladsummerno-oven

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta shells — uncooked
  • 2 ear sweet corn — kernels sliced off the cob
  • 1 pint grape tomatoes — halved
  • ⅔ lb zucchini — diced
  • ¼ cup red onion — diced
  • 1 whole avocado — diced
  • 1 whole lemon — 2 Tbsp juice and 1/2 tsp zest
  • ¼ cup olive oil — for the vinaigrette
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ¼ tsp dried basil
  • ¼ cup parsley — fresh, chopped
  • ⅛ tsp salt
  • ⅛ tsp black pepper

Steps

  1. Whisk the lemon juice, lemon zest, olive oil, basil, Dijon, salt, pepper, and parsley into a vinaigrette.
  2. Bring a large salted pot of water to a boil and cook the pasta shells until tender, about 7 minutes. Drain, rinse with cool water, and drain well.
  3. While the pasta cooks, slice the corn off the cobs, halve the tomatoes, dice the zucchini, dice the onion, and dice the avocado.
  4. Combine the cooled pasta and vegetables in a large bowl, pour the vinaigrette over, and toss. Season with salt to taste and serve.

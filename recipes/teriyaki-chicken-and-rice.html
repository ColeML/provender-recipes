One Pot Teriyaki Chicken and Rice

Serves 8 · 40 min total

asianchickenstovetopone-potquick

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breast — about 2 breasts, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 2 Tbsp cooking oil
  • 4 clove garlic — minced
  • 2 tsp fresh ginger — grated
  • 3 cup jasmine rice — uncooked
  • 5 cup water
  • 24 oz frozen stir fry vegetables
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • 4 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 4 ea green onions — sliced

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into 1/2-inch pieces.
  2. In a large deep pot or Dutch oven, heat the oil over medium. Add garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute, then add the chicken and saute just until the outside is no longer pink (it finishes cooking with the rice).
  3. Add the uncooked rice and saute 1-2 minutes. Add the water and stir briefly to loosen anything stuck to the bottom.
  4. Cover, bring to a boil over medium-high, then reduce to low and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile whisk the teriyaki sauce: soy sauce, brown sugar, and sesame oil.
  6. Sprinkle the frozen vegetables evenly over the top, cover, and cook on low 5 minutes more. Turn off the heat and rest, covered, 5 minutes.
  7. Drizzle the sauce over, then gently fold to coat, scooping to the bottom. Cover and rest 5 minutes. Top with green onions.
  8. Note: this is a doubled batch (8 servings) - use a large pot and give it a couple extra minutes to come to a boil. No oven.

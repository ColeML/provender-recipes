Ultimate BBQ Chicken Quesadillas

Serves 8 · 25 min total

tex-mexquickkid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 2½ cup rotisserie chicken — chopped, about 3/4 of a rotisserie chicken
  • 2 can (15oz) black beans — drained
  • ½ ea red onion
  • 2 ea jalapeno
  • ¾ cup cilantro
  • 3¼ cup cheddar cheese — shredded
  • 1½ tsp chili powder
  • ¾ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • ¾ cup bbq sauce
  • 16 ea flour tortillas — 7-inch

Steps

  1. Add the chopped rotisserie chicken and drained black beans to a large bowl.
  2. Finely dice the red onion. Remove the seeds from the jalapenos, then finely dice. Chop the cilantro.
  3. Add the onion, jalapenos, cilantro, shredded cheddar, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and BBQ sauce to the bowl. Stir until evenly combined.
  4. Place about 1/3 cup of the BBQ chicken mixture on each tortilla and spread over one half. Fold the tortilla closed.
  5. Cook the filled tortillas in a dry skillet over medium-low heat until the outside is browned and crispy and the cheese has melted.
  6. Cut each quesadilla in half and serve.

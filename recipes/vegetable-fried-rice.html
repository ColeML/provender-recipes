Vegetable Fried Rice

Serves 5 · 27 min total

asianstovetopvegetarian

Ingredients

  • 3 clove garlic — minced
  • 1¼ tsp ginger — grated
  • 1 ea carrot — diced
  • 1 ea red bell pepper — diced
  • 5 ea green onion — sliced
  • 3 large egg
  • 1¼ cup frozen peas
  • 3¾ tbsp cooking oil
  • 3¾ cup rice — cooked and cooled
  • 3¾ tbsp soy sauce
  • 1¼ tbsp sesame oil — toasted

Steps

  1. Mince the garlic, grate the ginger, dice the carrot and bell pepper, slice the green onions, measure the peas.
  2. Scramble the eggs in an oiled skillet, then set aside.
  3. Cook the carrots, then the bell pepper and white parts of the green onion, then the peas, in the same skillet. Set aside.
  4. Add oil, garlic, ginger, and the cooked rice to the skillet; stir and heat through.
  5. Pour in the soy sauce and sesame oil, stir to combine, then add back the eggs and vegetables. Top with the green ends of the onions.

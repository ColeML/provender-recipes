Watermelon and Feta Salad

Serves 6 · 20 min total

sidesaladno-cooksummer

Ingredients

  • ½ small watermelon — diced into 1/2-inch cubes, about 4 cups
  • ½ cup feta cheese — crumbled
  • 1 whole cucumber — peeled and diced
  • ½ whole red onion — finely julienned
  • 2 Tbsp fresh mint — minced
  • 2 Tbsp parsley — minced
  • 1 Tbsp white vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp balsamic glaze — for drizzling
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Steps

  1. Whisk the white vinegar, olive oil, salt, and pepper to make the dressing.
  2. Dice the watermelon into 1/2-inch cubes. Combine with the feta, cucumber, red onion, mint, and parsley in a large bowl.
  3. Pour the dressing over and toss gently so you don't mash the watermelon. Chill, covered, up to an hour to let the flavors meld.
  4. Drizzle each portion with balsamic glaze and add extra salt and pepper if needed.

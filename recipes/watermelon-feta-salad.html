Watermelon Feta Salad

Serves 5 · 20 min total

no-cookvegetarian

Ingredients

  • ¾ tbsp white vinegar
  • 5 tsp olive oil — pantry staple
  • ⅜ tsp salt — pantry staple
  • ⅜ tsp black pepper — pantry staple
  • 2 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • 3⅓ cup watermelon — diced
  • ½ cup feta cheese — crumbled
  • 1 ea cucumber — peeled and diced
  • ½ ea red onion — julienned
  • 2 tbsp mint — minced
  • 2 tbsp parsley — minced

Steps

  1. Whisk together the white vinegar, olive oil, salt, and pepper for the dressing.
  2. Dice the watermelon into 1/2-inch cubes.
  3. Combine the watermelon, feta, cucumber, red onion, mint, and parsley in a bowl.
  4. Pour the dressing over top and gently toss.
  5. Drizzle with balsamic glaze before serving.
